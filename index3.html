About Sila

Sila has been dancing her whole life — a choreographer, producer, performer, and fitness instructor who brings a rare depth of experience to every class, grounded equally in artistic practice and the joy of movement for everyone.

As Co-Artistic Director of Desert Diamonds NT, a cabaret dance theatre troupe based in the Northern Territory, Sila has performed at some of Australia's most celebrated festivals — Wide Open Spaces, FAB Alice Festival, Desert Festival, Darwin Festival, Darwin Fringe Festival and the Australian Burlesque Festival.

"After launching Zumba in Alice Springs, I saw half the town's population come through my classes in that first year alone. Those early days set the tone for everything that followed."

Sila discovered Zumba in 2010 and spent more than two years as a Zumba Jammer, travelling across Australia to teach choreography to fellow instructors and presenting alongside international Zumba presenters. Now based in Darwin, she brings an evolved but unmistakable energy to the floor — driven by great music, sexy rhythms, and classes that feel fun, welcoming and empowering. More than anything, she wants every person who walks into class to feel successful, confident and a little bit sexy too.

Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need dance experience?

For Zumba — absolutely not. Come as you are. Diamond Jazz suits anyone with some dance background, but if you're unsure, reach out and we'll help you decide.

What if I miss a week?

Life happens. Your place is held for the term regardless. We'll do our best to keep you up to date, especially in Diamond Jazz where we're building routines together.

Why only 15 places for Diamond Jazz?

Small classes mean you actually get better. The instructor can see you, correct you, and push you in a way that's impossible in a bigger group.

When does Term 3 start?

Term 3 runs Monday 10 August through to Monday 19 October 2025. There is no class on 14 September. That's 10 classes across the term — all included in your pass.

Can I upgrade my pass after I enrol?

Yes — if space is still available in the class you want to add. We recommend getting the Diamond Pass now if you're at all tempted by both.

Is this just for experienced dancers?

Not at all. BodyBeatz is for women who want to move, learn, and build something — whatever their starting point. The only requirement is showing up.
